How It Works

Five stages, clear roles, and one simple path through your work.

The five stages

  1. To DoWork you have not started yet.
  2. In ProgressWork you are doing right now.
  3. On HoldPaused. You must write a short reason.
  4. In ReviewFinished, and waiting for someone to check it.
  5. DoneChecked and approved. Nice work!

Roles and access

  1. AdminSets roles, projects, and access for everyone.
  2. Team leadAdds people and decides who can read or write each project.
  3. MemberWorks on projects with read or write access. Asks a lead for more.
  4. FilesAttach a file or a link to a task. Anyone with write access can add or remove them.
🙋

Stuck? Put the task On Hold and write a short note. That tells your team exactly what you need.
